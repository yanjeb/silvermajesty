OUR MISSION

Christian Life Center Tagum Campus exists to lead people into a growing relationship with Jesus Christ. We aim to build a community where people can experience God’s love, grow in faith, and discover their purpose.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Faith in Jesus Christ
We believe that Jesus Christ is the Son of God and the Savior of the world. Through Him we receive forgiveness, salvation, and new life.

Love for People
Our church values community and compassion. We believe every person matters and deserves to experience God’s love.

Growing in Faith
We encourage believers to grow spiritually through worship, discipleship, prayer, and service.

PASTORS & PRIMARY LEADERS

Our Pastors and Primary Leaders are committed to guiding the church with faith, wisdom, and servant leadership. Together they work to strengthen the ministry, support the congregation, and help spread the message of Jesus Christ in the community.
